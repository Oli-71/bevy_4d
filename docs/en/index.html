Hyperspace and Quantum Entanglement

How would our world look if we could observe it from a 4D perspective?
You can try it out here!

The simulation consists of six scenes. Please be sure to read the text first before experimenting with the parameters.

It remains to be seen whether higher spatial dimensions are just mental gymnastics, or whether they can — or even must — exist. See an essay on the absurdity of the quantum world. A 4D wormhole — as hinted at the end of the simulation — could solve the dilemma of non-locality...

I was inspired to create this simulation (programmed in Bevy/Rust) by the novel Quantum Space by Douglas Phillips and Edwin Abbott’s famous novella Flatland.

Have fun!
